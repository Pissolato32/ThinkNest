## States

## Rules

- Never ask more than one high-value question at a time.
- Stop asking as soon as a useful artifact can be generated.
- After every meaningful interaction:
  - update Project DNA
  - evaluate maturity
  - suggest one next action

## Grill-me

Questions adapt according to project maturity and project domain.

The objective is reducing uncertainty, not collecting information.
