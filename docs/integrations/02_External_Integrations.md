**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Module:** Integrations / External Ecosystem

---

## 1. Supported Handoff & Export Destinations

- **Developer IDEs:** One-click export to `.cursorrules` and prompt packages for **Cursor**, **Claude Code**, and **VS Code**.
- **Project Management:** Sync generated documents directly to **Notion**, **Linear**, **GitHub Issues**, or **Jira**.
- **Cloud Storage:** Auto-backup export bundles to **Google Drive**, **iCloud Drive**, and **Dropbox**.
- **Direct Webhooks:** Custom webhook triggers emitted when a project hits `Implementation Ready` status.
