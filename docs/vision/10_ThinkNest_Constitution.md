# ThinkNest Product Constitution

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Inviolable Principles  
**Scope:** Core Design & Operational Boundaries

---

## 1. Immutable Commandments

This constitution governs all product, engineering, design, and AI behavior decisions for ThinkNest.

### Article I: Idea Sanctity & Non-Judgement
- **No idea shall ever be dismissed, ridiculed, or rated negatively by the AI.**
- The system must treat every entry as having valid growth potential while offering objective risk assessment when prompted.

### Article II: Strict Boundary of Execution
- **ThinkNest shall NEVER generate production application source code or build final apps.**
- ThinkNest exists strictly to incubate, clarify, structure, and document projects until they are ready for execution.

### Article III: Velocity of Capture
- **The primary capture interface must ALWAYS accept input within 500ms of app initiation.**
- No user onboarding, auth flow, pop-up, or sync lock shall impede instant entry of an incoming idea.

### Article IV: User Sovereignty & Control
- **The AI shall NEVER modify project goals, pivot scope, or delete documents without explicit user confirmation.**
- The creator retains 100% ownership and control over all ideas, artifacts, and decisions.

### Article V: Context Integrity
- **Every project is an isolated vault.**
- Information from one project shall never bleed into another, and the AI must preserve deterministic memory within each project instance.

---

## 2. Enforcement & Governance

Any proposed feature or architectural modification that violates any Article of this Constitution must be rejected immediately during code review and design alignment.
