# Grill-me Engine

Goal:
Reduce uncertainty.

Algorithm

Capture
↓
Identify ambiguity
↓
Ask one question
↓
Receive answer
↓
Can a valuable artifact be generated?

YES -> Generate artifact.
NO -> Ask next question.

Stopping Rules

Stop immediately after enough information exists for:
- PRD
- Roadmap
- Business Plan
- Research
- Architecture
- Outline

Never interrogate the user.
Conversation should feel collaborative.
